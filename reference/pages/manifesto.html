your time

Less managing.
More living.

This afternoon, you have an idea. You find a quiet moment, open your laptop, and think you’ll finally get to it today.

Then there’s a reply to write. A meeting to rearrange. A document somewhere in that thread. Before you know it, the afternoon is slipping away and the idea is still sitting where you left it.

yours again.

We know that day.
We’ve lived it too.

It’s why we’re building Merlin, an AI assistant for your Mac. Something you can turn to in the middle of all this and say, “Can you give me a hand?”

So this time, you ask. Merlin finds the details in your connected apps. You open Companion beside the email you’re stuck on, work out a reply together, and move on.

When your next meeting starts, you let Merlin take notes. Someone says something that sparks a thought, and you’re there to catch it. For once, you aren’t trying to listen and write at the same time.

less on your mind

And there it is.
A little room.

After the call, there’s a trip to sort out. Merlin looks through flights in your browser and helps with the booking, stopping for your approval before you commit. One more thing off your mind.

You come back to that idea. You explain it, change your mind, try another direction. With Merlin, the first version becomes a deck, a spreadsheet, or a working website. Something you can open and keep making yours.

more room for life

Before you finish, you set up a routine for your next morning briefing. The familiar work has a place to go. It doesn’t all have to live in your head when you step away.

Then you close your laptop. There’s some of the day left for you. You take the long way home, call someone back, or do absolutely nothing for a while. We’re building Merlin for this part, too.

Your time.
Yours to spend.
