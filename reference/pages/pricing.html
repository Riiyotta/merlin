A few things
you might be wondering.

Which plan should I choose?

Choose based on how much you expect to use Merlin. Pro gives you 25,000 monthly credits for everyday help. Ultra gives you 85,000 for more involved work, and Max gives you 250,000 for frequent use and bigger projects. All three include the capabilities above.

What are credits?

Credits cover Merlin’s AI usage, including model calls and paid tools. The amount a task uses depends on the model, tools, and work involved, so one message doesn’t always use the same number of credits. Follow your balance and recent activity in Settings → Usage.

Do unused credits roll over?

Unused included credits don’t roll over. Your allowance refreshes each month, and you can see your renewal date in Settings → Usage.

What happens if I run out?

When your balance reaches zero, new paid work won’t start. You can check your remaining credits and renewal date in Settings → Usage, and manage your subscription in Settings → Billing.

Is there an annual option?

Yes. Annual subscriptions save 20% on the subscription price. You receive the same monthly credit allowance as the equivalent monthly plan, and credits refresh every month. You can review the annual plan details in the app before subscribing.

How do I get started?

Download Merlin for Mac and follow the setup in the app. Your trial starts with 2,000 credits to explore. You can choose your plan and manage billing in the app.
